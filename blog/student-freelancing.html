A student's guide to freelancing while studying IT

One of the best ways to begin freelancing is by offering a small, clear service. Pick one area that you can confidently complete, then package it simply.

Student schedules can be unpredictable, so timing matters. Use simple written agreements, fixed milestones, and a realistic turnaround estimate.

That reliable experience builds confidence faster than trying to sell everything at once.
