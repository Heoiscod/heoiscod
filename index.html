PROJECT 01 / BOOKING SYSTEM

Pickle Social - Cebu

A convenient online way for the Pickle Social Cebu community to book a pickleball court.

LIVE PROJECT picklesocialcebu.site VISIT SITE ↗

ABOUT THE PROJECT

A booking system for Pickle Social Cebu, helping its pickleball community arrange bookings in one place.

LOCATION

It is made for the pickleball community in Cebu, Philippines. The specific venue can be added when confirmed.

DEVELOPMENT

I developed it as a digital booking system for Pickle Social Cebu. The goal was to make arranging a booking straightforward for the pickleball community.

LESSONS LEARNED

  • A booking flow should make the next step clear to users.
  • Keep booking details easy to review before users confirm.
  • Design for mobile so users can make bookings wherever they are.
